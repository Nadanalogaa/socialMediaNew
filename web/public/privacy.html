1. Who this covers

3. Data from Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

When you connect a Facebook Page or Instagram Business account, you grant our application permission through Meta's own consent screen. We then store:

  • The Page or account ID, name and profile picture
  • An access token, encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM
  • Posts we published for you, and their engagement figures
  • Comments on those posts, where you have asked us to manage replies

We use this data only to provide features you have asked for. We never post without an explicit action from you, and we do not access Pages or accounts you have not connected.

Revoking access on Facebook immediately invalidates our tokens. Meta notifies us, and we delete the stored connection.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and content: while your account is active
  • After you close your account: deleted within 30 days
  • Access tokens: deleted immediately when you disconnect a channel
  • Billing records: retained as long as Indian tax law requires

6. Deleting your data

You can remove data at any time:

  • Disconnect a channel in Channels — its tokens are deleted at once
  • Delete individual posts from your dashboard
  • Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] to close your account entirely
  • Remove our app in your Facebook settings — Meta tells us, and we delete the connection

If you have submitted a deletion request through Facebook, you can check its status at /data-deletion using the confirmation code you were given.

7. Security

  • Passwords are hashed with scrypt and never stored in readable form
  • Platform access tokens are encrypted at rest and are never sent to your browser
  • All traffic is served over HTTPS
  • Each organisation's data is isolated, and access is checked on every request

8. Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will respond within 30 days.
